Welcome to PIG!

Rules:

(1) PIG can have a minimum of TWO players and maximum of FOUR.
(2) Each player rolls a six-sided die.
(3) Rolling of any number OTHER THAN 1 increases their total score by that number. The current player can choose to continue/discontinue the game, keeping their current score.
(4) If the rolled number IS 1, the total score goes down to ZERO and the player loses their chance. The next player starts their turn.
(5) The game is continued till one of the players reaches a score GREATER THAN OR EQUAL TO 50.
(6) All other players get one last turn until the winner is determined.
